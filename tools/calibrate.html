Swish calibration

Offline mode: runs the same pose + metric code as the app on your reference clips (suggested: 2 clips from each of 4 players, clean side-on, 60 fps or higher, portrait) and proposes ranges = observed range plus a buffer. Nothing is uploaded; it all runs in this page.

Each clip is analysed from s for up to 5 s.

Proposed ranges

Copy into config/settings.json under moves.shooting.ranges (or download and replace). Edit freely afterwards.
